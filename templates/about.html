The story

Why this exists.

TineTech started because good websites are still surprisingly rare.

Too many businesses are stuck with slow, bloated sites that cost too much to update and do too little to actually help. Or they're sold a web app that solves the wrong problem.

We started TineTech to do the opposite: small, focused work — designed properly, built cleanly, delivered on time.

No jargon. No inflated scope. No surprise invoices. Just a studio that treats your project like it matters.

How we think

Four principles.
No exceptions.

These aren't slogans. They're the filter we run every decision through.

01

Clarity over cleverness

Simple beats complicated. Every time. If a feature doesn't help the user, it doesn't ship.

02

Speed is a feature

Fast sites convert better, rank better, and respect people's time. We treat performance as non-negotiable.

03

Say it straight

Honest timelines. Honest pricing. If something's not a fit, we'll tell you rather than take the work.

04

Built to last

Clean code, modern tools, no lock-in. Your site should be easier to maintain in two years, not harder.

Honest fit

What we don't do.

Knowing what to say no to is just as important as knowing what to build.

×

Cheap template sites

We build custom, not from a theme. If you want something quick and generic, there are better options than us.

×

Vague retainers

No monthly fees for unclear work. You pay for defined projects, or defined support — never for "hours."

×

Rushing to ship

If a deadline forces sloppy work, we'd rather reset the timeline than hand you something we're not proud of.

×

Locking you in

Your code, your data, your accounts. We set things up so you're never dependent on us to stay online.
